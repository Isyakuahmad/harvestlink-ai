# Product specification

HarvestLink AI reduces friction between agricultural supply and demand by helping participants discover, verify, match and coordinate produce, buyers, transport and storage.

## Initial users
Farmers and cooperatives; buyers, aggregators, wholesalers and processors; transporters; warehouse operators; platform administrators.

## Interfaces
WhatsApp for accessible conversational workflows; web dashboard for listings, buyer requirements, coordination and administration.

## Planned capabilities
1. Onboarding and organization profiles.
2. Produce listings with quantity, unit, quality, location, availability window and evidence.
3. Buyer requirements and structured matching.
4. Market observations with source, location, unit and timestamp.
5. Transport and storage discovery and quotations.
6. Transaction and delivery status tracking.
7. Verification, moderation and audit history.

Never present indicative prices as confirmed offers. Never invent buyers, stock, prices, transport or capacity. Require human approval before offers, purchases, payments or binding commitments. Measure verified matches and completed deliveries.


## Farmer Activity & Evidence Ledger
Farmers can create farm profiles, seasonal crop records, dated activity and expense entries, and attach evidence references. The platform labels records as self-reported, document-supported or independently verified without conflating those levels. Farmers can generate private season reports and grant named recipients time-limited access to a selected season or all seasons; grants are revocable and report access is logged.

Reports support loan, grant, cooperative finance and agricultural programme applications. They are supporting records only, not audited accounts, a credit score, an eligibility determination or a promise of funding. WhatsApp/voice extraction, secure file uploads and PDF/CSV exports are subsequent delivery items.
